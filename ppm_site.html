What are PPMs?

Psychological Profile Maps (PPMs) are psychological questionnaire and journaling tools. It helps you lay out your information for a clinician to review and for you to progressively make changes to as your treatment progresses.

Use Case Basis

Mental health professionals deal with a lot of cases which cannot possibly be generalized under the constraints of modern psychology. PPMs help physicians formulate cases with their patients, by condensing relevant information into an intuitive chart.

The objective of PPMs is to reduce wasted time, money and effort in therapies and prescriptions that aren't relevant, or may even be harmful. For example, a well-formulated PPM can help a mental health professional differentiate serotonin-based depression symptoms from dopamine-related depression. In some cases, this will result in an erroneous diagnosis of "treatment-resistant depression" and a misprescription of serotonin-related drugs (such as SSRIs), which can cause long-term neurological and psychological harm to the patient.
